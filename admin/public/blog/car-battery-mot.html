Car battery health and your MOT

Spot failure early — Palmers Green fitting service.

← Back to Blog · 3 May 2026

Most batteries last three to five years depending on climate, short journeys and electrical load. Stop–start systems demand stronger units.

Symptoms of a weak battery

  • Engine turns over slowly or needs multiple attempts
  • Dim headlights at idle
  • Infotainment resets or warning messages
  • Corrosion on terminals

MOT connection

The MOT includes checks that need a healthy electrical system — for example lamps, wipers and sometimes emissions equipment. A flat battery on the day delays testing; some faults trace to low voltage.
