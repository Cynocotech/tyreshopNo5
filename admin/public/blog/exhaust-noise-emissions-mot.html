Exhaust noise, leaks and MOT emissions

Repairs in Bourne Hill and North London.

← Back to Blog · 3 May 2026

The exhaust system carries gases away from the engine, reduces noise and houses catalytic converters and filters on many vehicles. Defects affect noise, emissions and safety.

Common MOT issues

  • Leaks before the catalytic converter or excessive corrosion
  • Loose or missing mounts allowing contact with the body
  • Excessive smoke indicating engine wear or turbo faults
  • Noise clearly above original design levels

Petrol vs diesel

Diesel particulate filters and EGR systems add complexity. Short urban journeys can block DPFs; forced regeneration or repair may be needed before emissions pass.
