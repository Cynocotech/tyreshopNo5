MOT class for cars vs vans (Class A & B)

Book the correct DVSA test for your vehicle in London.

← Back to Blog

3 May 2026 · Bourn Hill Tyre & MOT | London

Not every MOT is the same. Passenger cars and light commercial vehicles use different MOT classes and limits. Choosing the wrong booking wastes time and can mean an invalid test for your van or pickup.

What is MOT Class A (cars)?

Class A covers standard passenger cars — the everyday hatchbacks, saloons and estates most drivers use. The test follows DVSA rules for safety items such as brakes, lights, steering, tyres, visibility and emissions. At Bourn Hill we carry out Class A MOTs for cars with clear pricing and same-week appointments where possible.

What is MOT Class B (vans)?

Class B applies to light commercial vehicles — many vans, pickups and crew cabs used for trade or deliveries. Weight limits and equipment checks can differ from a car. If you use a van for work, always tell the garage your registration so we confirm the correct class before the test.

Why the right class matters

  • Avoid an incorrect test that does not match your vehicle’s DVLA category.
  • Keep insurance and road-use compliance straightforward.
  • Plan maintenance: vans often wear brakes and tyres faster than cars.
