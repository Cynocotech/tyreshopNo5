Full service, major service or MOT only?

Choose the right work for your mileage and budget.

← Back to Blog · 3 May 2026

An MOT is a legal minimum safety and emissions inspection. It is not a service and does not replace oil changes, filters or manufacturer schedules.

MOT only

Best when the certificate is due soon and you maintain the car elsewhere — or you recently had a full service. You receive a pass or fail against DVSA standards only.

Full service

Typically annual (or by mileage). Includes oil and filter change, checks on brakes, tyres, fluids, lights and safety items. Helps reliability and can surface problems before they become MOT failures.

Major service

Deeper than a full service: extra filters, plugs where due, and broader inspection. Often aligned to manufacturer long-interval schedules.

Why book MOT + service together

  • One visit and one handover — less downtime.
  • Mechanics can rectify advisories before the certificate expires.
